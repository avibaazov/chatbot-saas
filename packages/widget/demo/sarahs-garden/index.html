About us

Sarah's Garden is a family-run flower shop founded by Sarah Whitcombe in 2014. We grow about 70% of our stems on our own half-acre plot just outside Ashford, and buy the rest from two nearby farms. Everything is cut to order, so a bouquet you buy on a Tuesday was almost always still in the ground on Monday. We do not sell imported roses or dyed flowers.

Opening hours & delivery

The shop on Bank Street is open Tuesday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. We are closed Sundays and Mondays.

There is no delivery on Sundays or Mondays, so a Sunday order arrives on Tuesday. Letterbox flowers are sent by Royal Mail first class and reach most of mainland Britain in two to three days.

Caring for your flowers

  • Trim 2cm off each stem at an angle before putting them in water.
  • Use the sachet of flower food and change the water every two days.
  • Keep the vase out of direct sun and away from the fruit bowl — ripening fruit gives off ethylene, which wilts blooms fast.
  • Tulips keep growing after cutting and bend toward the light; turn the vase daily if you want them straight.
  • Strip any leaves that would sit below the waterline so the water stays clear.

A hand-tied bouquet from us should last 7 to 10 days with this care.

Our freshness guarantee

If your flowers wilt or drop within 5 days of delivery, send us a photo and we will replace the bouquet or refund it in full — your choice, no need to return anything. Because the flowers are perishable we cannot take back an order simply because you changed your mind, but if something is wrong we will always put it right. Subscription payments can be stopped from your account page and any unshipped month is refunded.
